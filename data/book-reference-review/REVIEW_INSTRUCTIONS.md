# Linking exam questions to the reference book (main book.pdf)

You link real Iranian driving-theory exam questions to the page of the official textbook
("کتاب آموزشی هنرجویان پایه سوم") that supports their correct answer.

## Inputs
- Your batch: (generated by scripts/tiering/retrieve_candidates.py) — a JSON array. Each item has: id, text (question), options,
  correctOptionId, correctAnswerText, image (path relative to the project root, or null), imageAlt, and
  candidates = pages proposed by a lexical search (bookPage, chapterId, headings, score, passages).
  The candidates are only suggestions and are often wrong or incomplete.
- Full book transcription (verbatim, read visually from the page images): data/book-pages.json
  → {"pages":[{bookPage, bookPdfPage, chapterId, headings, text, signs:[{caption,description}], figures:[...]}]}
  Search it with grep/python (e.g. python3 -c "import json;..." or grep -o on a keyword) whenever the candidates
  do not clearly contain the answer.
- Section index: data/book-structure.json → "sections" (title, chapterId, startBookPage, endBookPage).
- Book chapters by printed page: ch1 2–76 (قوانین و مقررات), ch2 77–113 (رانندگی ایمن), ch3 114–156 (فنی خودرو),
  ch4 157–188 (فرهنگ رانندگی), ch5 189–201 (امداد و نجات), ch6 202–219 (آلودگی‌های ترافیک).
  Signs catalogue: book pages 28–45 (warning 29–32, regulatory 33–38, informational 39–41, route 42,
  supplementary 43, local/work-zone 44, stickers 45). Colours/shapes of signs: 20–21. Road markings: 22–27.
- Question images live under assets/questions/ — look at one (Read tool) only when the text alone
  cannot tell you what is asked (e.g. an image-option question whose stem is generic).

## Hard rules
- NEVER guess a page number. Give a page only after reading that page's transcription and confirming it covers
  the question's subject. Accuracy matters more than coverage: when unsure, use "low" or "not_found".
- Do not change, question or re-solve the correct answer. It is fixed. If the book seems to contradict it,
  still reference the relevant page and add note "book_conflict: ..." (and use at most "medium").
- supportingText must be copied VERBATIM from the chosen page's transcription (its "text", a sign "caption",
  or a figure "caption"), max ~220 characters, the shortest span that shows why the page answers the question.
  Do not add ellipses in the middle, do not fix typos, do not merge distant sentences. It will be checked
  programmatically as an exact substring (after whitespace/ZWNJ normalisation).
- bookReference: short Persian label of the section, preferably the printed heading that covers the page
  (e.g. «تابلوهای اخطاری», «مفهوم رنگ ها در علائم عبور و مرور», «خطوط هاشور»). No page numbers inside it.

## Confidence
- high      the page explicitly states the rule / number / definition / sign caption that makes the correct answer right.
- medium    the page clearly covers the same subject and supports the answer, but not word-for-word
            (answer requires a small inference, or the sign/figure matches but wording differs).
- low       only topically related; the specific answer is not stated on the page.
- not_found nothing in the book covers the question. bookPage must be null.

## Output
Write data/book-reference-review/result-NN.json (same NN as your batch) — a JSON array with one object per question:
{"id": "...", "bookPage": 29 | null, "confidence": "high|medium|low|not_found",
 "supportingText": "..." | null, "bookReference": "..." | null,
 "secondaryPages": [..] (0–2 other pages that ALSO directly support the answer; usually []),
 "chapterId": 1–6 | null, "chapterBasis": "page" | "topic" | null, "note": null | "short note"}
- chapterId: the chapter of bookPage ("page"). If not_found but the subject clearly belongs to one chapter
  (e.g. a first-aid question → 5, pollution → 6, engine/tyres/brakes technical → 3, behaviour/culture/psychology → 4,
  a traffic sign or a traffic law → 1, defensive/safe-driving technique → 2) give that chapter with "topic"; otherwise null.
- Save progress incrementally: rewrite the result file after every ~20 questions. If the result file already
  exists when you start, keep its entries and continue with the ids that are missing (resume support).
- Every id of the batch must appear exactly once in the final file.

Finish with a very short report: number of questions per confidence level and any systematic problems.
